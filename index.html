    Match settings

    3 stocks, 7 minute timer, items off, stage hazards off.

    Game 1 on Battlefield or Final Destination. In a best of 3, the loser of each game picks the next stage.

    Both players come up together to report who won.

    How places work

    1st through 4th come straight out of the bracket. Double elimination leaves a tie for 5th, so those two players play one extra match to settle it.
